BEFORE YOU GO UNDER

  • This is dry practice only. Never do breath-hold training in or near water, in the bath, while driving, or standing up.
  • Sit or lie down somewhere safe. Never push toward blackout. Belly twitches (contractions) are normal. Dizziness, tingling, or tunnel vision mean stop and breathe.
  • Don't hyperventilate before a hold. Calm, slow breaths only.
  • Skip the breath-hold chapters (IV–VII) if you're pregnant or have a heart, lung, or blood-pressure condition, or epilepsy. Chapters I–III are gentle breathing and fine for most people.
  • Do at most one breath-hold table a day.
